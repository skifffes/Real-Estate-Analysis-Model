Abstract:
The real estate industry is an important part of the macro-economy, and the research on the
development of the real estate industry is an important part of the macro-economics. This paper uses the data
envelopment analysis (DEA) model to explore the input-output efficiency level of the real estate industry in
Shaanxi Province; in-depth analysis of its annual trend, combined with the conclusion of the average value of
real estate input-output under different element combinations, to explore the important factors affecting the re
－
gional real estate industry, the results show that: the comprehensive efficiency value of 2009-2012 and 20172019 is equal to 1, which shows that the input-output of the real estate industry in Shaanxi Province is effi
－
cient in these seven years, and the comprehensive efficiency of the real estate industry in other years is less
than 1, indicating that the input-output of the real estate industry from 2013 to 2016 is inefficient. The input
of real estate employment in Shaanxi Province has a significant impact on its output.
